Where each problem stands

From engineering’s update on #4471 (closed 5 October):

  • parser: fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed as completed.
  • hint: won’t fix, because the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • one-day: not in the update. No fix, so its ticket stays open. Reports like these will keep coming.

The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first, and day-first tenants write the day first. Day and month swap.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way. People type what the hint says. Day and month swap again, but the fix is in the form. These are reports through the form channel from day-first tenants on booking-v3.
  • one-day: the date moves one day earlier with no swap, e.g. Tuesday 12 May shown as Monday 11 May. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The cause is not verified. It looks like a time-zone conversion of a date-only booking. Engineering has to check it in sched-prod.

How this was counted

  • One report per id. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice.
  • Placed by tenant locale, form and channel (causes.ts), not by the intake tag. The intake tag guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant often.
  • One-day moves come from the desk’s notes. As on #4471, they stay counted where they are filed (parser), and have their own row and ticket.
